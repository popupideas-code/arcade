Meridian Group watches the whole city through the Halo grid. You are Wren, a courier, and the drive in your jacket proves it. Sable is waiting on the Meridian Tower roof. Halo drones own the streets, so stay above them.

  • SteerLeft and Right buttons, or arrow keys
  • JumpJump button, or Space. Also climbs and wall runs
  • SlideSlide button, or Down
  • PulsePulse button, or E. Knocks out drones. Collect blue orbs to charge it
  • HideTunnels and steam hide you from drones
