Welcome, explorer! 🚀

You're parked at our Sun. Every bright star you see is real and sits where astronomers measured it.

1 · Pick a place

Tap a star, a glowing nebula, a 🕳 black hole, or a ◇ sci-fi world. Or press ☰ Places.

2 · Pick a ship

Each ship uses a different way to cross space: real light sails and rockets, theoretical warp drives and wormholes, or pure sci-fi jumps. Tap the ship button to read how each one works.

3 · Watch the two clocks

τ is your clock. t is Earth's clock. Fly close to light speed and Earth's clock races ahead of yours: that's Einstein's time dilation.

4 · Hunt for planets

In ⋯ → 🪐 Planet Hunter, find planets the way TESS does: spot the tiny dips in a star's light when a planet passes in front.

5 · Explore

When you arrive, press Enter system to fly around the planets in 3D. Try 🌌 Galaxy map to see where you are in the Milky Way.
