Welcome, explorer! 🚀

You're parked at our Sun. Every bright star you see is real and sits where astronomers measured it.

1 · Pick a place

Tap a star, a glowing nebula, a black hole, or a ◇ sci-fi world. Or open 📍 Places at the bottom.

2 · Pick a ship

Every ship flies close to the speed of light, each with a different engine: light sails, antimatter, fusion, even a tiny black hole. 🚀 Ship at the bottom to read how each one works.

3 · Watch the two clocks

τ is your clock. t is Earth's clock. At 99% of light speed, Earth's clock races 7 times faster than yours: that's Einstein's time dilation.

4 · Hunt for planets

In 🪐 Hunt, find planets the way TESS does: spot the tiny dips in a star's light when a planet passes in front.

5 · Explore

When you arrive, press Enter system to fly around the planets in 3D. Tap 🌌 (top right) to see where you are in the Milky Way.
